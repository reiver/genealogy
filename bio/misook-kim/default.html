Misook Kim (김미숙)

my mother-in-law

by Charles Iliya Krempeaux

Misook Kim (김미숙) is my mother-in-law. She is the mother of my wife — Hyerim Kang (강혜림)

I became aware of Misook Kim in the year 2018. And, I first met Misook Kim (김미숙) the year 2019 after Hyerim Kang (강혜림) got engaged.

Before meeting Misook Kim (김미숙), I spend about 1 month practicing (with Hyerim Kang (강혜림)) how to ask her mother for permission to marry her daughter.

Relationship

I really like (my mother-in-law) Misook Kim (김미숙). And, I have felt (emotionally) close to her for a long time.

My spoken Korean is very limited — probably at the level of a 2 year old. And, (my mother-in-law's) Misook Kim's English is very limited — she knows some words and phrases. But, we found ways to communicate — mostly by using translation technology.

There have been times when I felt I could not talk to my wife (Hyerim Kang (강혜림)) about something, where I still talked to her mother (Misook Kim (김미숙)) about it. (Not to have an ally, but to just have someone to talk to.)

Halmeoni (할머니)

Misook Kim (김미숙) is my children's maternal grandmother.

Misook Kim (김미숙) has been part of my children's lives. With the exception of when the COVID-19 lockdowns were taking places, my children have so far spent time with her her every year their lives — for at least 1 month, and sometimes longer.

My children call her: halmi (할미).

Halmi (할미) basically means "grandma". And similar to how "grandma" is a shortened, affectionate, and informal way of saying "grandmother" — halmi (할미) is a shortened, affectionate, and informal way of saying halmeoni (할머니)).
